Device setup · About 2 minutes

Your league.
One tap away.

Save Mitch Fantasy League to your Home Screen, then turn on push notifications. No App Store download needed.

Android & desktop

On Android, open MFL in Chrome, use the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen when offered. Open the app, choose your team, then use the bell to enable notifications.

On desktop, use the bell in a supported browser and allow notifications. Installation is optional. If blocked, change this site's notification permission in your browser settings.

Choose alert categories and quiet hours in the notification panel. Use Test notification to check this device. Repeat setup on each device; settings and saved trade comparisons are browser-specific.

Before you start: Use an iPhone running iOS 16.4 or later for push notifications. Start in Safari.

  1. Open the league in Safari

    Visit mitch-fantasy.netlify.app. If you opened this guide inside a group chat or another app, copy the league link and paste it into Safari.

  2. Tap Share

    Look for the square with an upward arrow in Safari’s toolbar. Depending on your iOS version and layout, you may need to open the More (…) menu first.

    Share

  3. Add to Home Screen

    Scroll down the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen. If it’s missing, check Edit Actions. Keep Open as Web App on if that option appears, then tap Add.

    Add to Home Screen

  4. Open MFL from your Home Screen

    Leave Safari and tap the new MFL icon on your iPhone’s Home Screen. Continue inside this installed app to enable push notifications.

    Your new league shortcut

  5. Turn on notifications

    Inside MFL, tap the bell. Choose your team for matchup alerts (optional), tap Enable notifications, then tap Allow on the iPhone permission prompt.

    Bell → Enable notifications → Allow

  6. Check you’re all set

    The notification panel should say Notifications are on. You can close it and use the league as usual. Repeat setup on each device where you want alerts.

Need a hand?

I tapped “Don’t Allow”

Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → MFL (or the name you used when adding it), then turn on Allow Notifications. Return to MFL from your Home Screen and tap the bell, then Enable notifications again to finish registering this device.

I don’t see a permission prompt

Make sure you opened MFL from its Home Screen icon, rather than a Safari tab. Check Settings → General → About → iOS Version; push requires iOS 16.4 or later. If permission is already allowed, a second prompt may not appear; check the confirmation in the bell panel.

Notifications are on, but alerts aren’t showing

Check Settings → Notifications → MFL for notification permission and Lock Screen, Notification Center, and Banner options. Check whether a Focus mode or Scheduled Summary is silencing or delaying alerts. Turning alerts on does not send a test notification.
